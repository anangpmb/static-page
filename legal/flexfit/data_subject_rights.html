Data Subject Rights

Last updated: October 26, 2026 · Effective date: October 26, 2026

The short version

  • You have rights over your personal data: to know, access, correct, delete, restrict, object, take it with you, and withdraw consent.
  • FlexFit Watch keeps your data on your watch and your phone, not on our servers, so you can exercise most rights yourself, right away, as shown below.
  • For anything you have sent us (such as an email or recorded sets), write to secretbaseprimary@gmail.com. It is free.

1. About this page

This page explains the rights you have over your personal data when you use FlexFit Watch (com.flexfit.watch, the "App"), and how to exercise them. It complements our Privacy Policy, which describes what data the App handles and where it goes.

These rights come from data protection laws such as:

  • Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP, Law No. 27 of 2022);
  • the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR;
  • the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA (CCPA);
  • Brazil's LGPD, and other laws that give similar rights.

Which rights apply, and exactly how, depends on where you live. We extend the rights below to every user, wherever they live.

2. Where your data is

The App has no account, no internet connection, no ads and no analytics. Your workouts, calibration, preferences and recorded sets are stored on your watch, and once sent, in the FlexFit app on your phone. We do not hold a copy.

That means you are always in control: the tools to see, change and delete your data are on your devices. The only personal data we may hold is what you choose to send us, such as an email or recorded sets.

3. Your rights and how to exercise them

Right What it means How to exercise it
To be informed Know what data is processed, why, how and by whom. Read our Privacy Policy. Ask us if anything is unclear.
Access See the personal data held about you, and get a copy. On the watch, Finish shows every set and rep of a workout. On the phone, open any "Watch workout" in FlexFit. Recorded sets are readable CSV files in Download/FlexFit/recordings/. For data you sent us, email us.
Rectification (correction) Have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected. After each set, correct the reps with − and +, set the weight, and pick how it felt, before moving on. Your corrections also retrain the counter for you. If a workout already on your phone is wrong, delete it in the FlexFit phone app and log it again there, or email us for help.
Erasure (deletion) Have your data deleted. Discard a recorded set before saving it. Finish a workout without sets to drop it. Delete workouts in the FlexFit phone app (tick them, then delete) and recordings from Download/FlexFit/recordings/. Uninstall the App from the watch to delete all its data, including calibration and preferences. For data you sent us, email us.
Restriction of processing Limit how your data is used. The sensors are read only while you do a set. Do not set up phone sync, or keep the FlexFit phone app closed, and workouts stay on the watch only.
Objection Object to processing, including for direct marketing or profiling. We do no marketing or profiling. You can stop any processing by not starting a set, revoking the permissions below, or uninstalling the App.
Data portability Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format and move it elsewhere. Workouts reach your phone as JSON files and recorded sets as CSV files: both open formats. The FlexFit phone app can export everything it keeps to one zip file with Profile → Backup.
Withdraw consent Take back consent you gave, at any time, without affecting what was done before. Revoke the App's sensor permissions in the watch's settings or the Huawei Health app. Revoke FlexFit's access to your watch in the Huawei Health app on your phone. Stop using Record a set.
Automated decision-making Not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing that significantly affect you. The App's only automated output is the rep count and the effort guess ("Easy / Solid / Maxed"). They are suggestions you confirm or change after every set, and have no legal or similar effect on you.
Non-discrimination Not to be treated differently for exercising your rights. The App works the same for everyone. We will never charge you or limit the App because you exercised a right.
Opt out of sale or sharing Stop your data being sold or shared for advertising (CCPA). Nothing to do: we do not sell or share your personal data, and the App contains no advertising.
Compensation Claim compensation for a violation of your data rights (for example UU PDP, Article 12). Contact us first so we can make it right; you may also bring a claim under the law that applies to you.
Complaint Complain to a data protection authority. See section 7.

4. Managing permissions

  • Accelerometer and gyroscope: without them the App cannot count reps. You can revoke them in the watch's settings, or in the Huawei Health app on your phone, where the watch's app permissions are listed.
  • The phone app's access to your watch: the FlexFit phone app asks Huawei Health for access to your watch the first time. You can revoke it in the Huawei Health app. Workouts then stay on the watch until you allow it again or uninstall the App.

The names of the settings can differ between watch models and Huawei Health versions.

5. Sending us a request

For anything you cannot do yourself on your devices, or for any data you have sent us, email us at secretbaseprimary@gmail.com with the subject "Data subject request – FlexFit Watch". Please include:

  • which right you want to exercise;
  • what data it concerns (for example "the recorded sets I emailed on 3 March");
  • the email address you used to contact us before, if any;
  • if you act for someone else, proof that you are allowed to (for example a signed authorization, or proof that you are the parent or guardian of a minor).

You can use this template:

Subject: Data subject request – FlexFit Watch

Right I want to exercise: [access / correction / deletion / restriction /
                           objection / portability / withdraw consent / other]
Data it concerns:         [describe]
Email I used before:      [if any]
Acting for someone else:  [no / yes, and on whose behalf]

6. How we handle requests

  • Free of charge. We do not charge for a request. If requests are clearly unfounded or excessive, we may refuse or ask a reasonable fee where the law allows it, and we will tell you why.
  • Verification. Since there is no account, we confirm a request by replying to the email address the data came from. We ask only for what we need to confirm it is you, and never for passwords.
  • Timing. We acknowledge your request within 3 days and answer within the time the law that applies to you sets, for example 3 × 24 hours for access and correction under UU PDP, one month under the GDPR (extendable by two months for complex requests, with notice), and 45 days under the CCPA.
  • If we cannot act. If we hold no data about you, which is the usual case, we will tell you so, and explain how to exercise the right on your devices. If we refuse a request, we will give our reasons and how you can challenge them.

7. Complaints

If you think your data has not been handled properly, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, for example:

  • Indonesia: the personal data protection agency established under UU PDP, or, until it is in operation, the Ministry of Communication and Digital Affairs (Komdigi);
  • EEA: the supervisory authority of the country where you live or work, or where the issue happened (list of EU authorities);
  • United Kingdom: the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk);
  • California: the California Privacy Protection Agency (cppa.ca.gov).

8. Data held by Huawei

The connection between your watch and your phone, and the distribution of the App, are provided by Huawei (Huawei Health, Wear Engine, HMS Core and HUAWEI AppGallery). For data Huawei holds about you, exercise your rights with Huawei directly, as described in its privacy statement: consumer.huawei.com/en/privacy/privacy-policy.
