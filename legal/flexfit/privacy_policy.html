💪 FlexFit Watch

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026 · Effective date: October 26, 2026

The short version

  • FlexFit Watch has no account, no internet connection, no ads and no analytics.
  • It uses the watch's motion sensors only while you are doing a set, to count your reps on the watch itself.
  • Your workouts are stored on the watch and, when you save them, sent only to the FlexFit app on your own phone, through Huawei Health.
  • We (the developer) never receive your workouts or sensor data. We have no server that stores them.
  • You can correct, discard or delete your data at any time. See your data subject rights.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how FlexFit Watch (bundle com.flexfit.watch, the "App") handles information when you use it. The App is a HUAWEI lite wearable app (such as the HUAWEI WATCH FIT 4) that logs a gym workout set by set, counts your repetitions, estimates how hard each set was, and sends the finished workout to the FlexFit app on your phone (com.flexfit.mobile).

The App is developed by FlexFit ("we", "us", "our"). In this policy, "your devices" means your watch and the phone paired with it.

By installing or using the App, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the App.

2. The data the App handles

Everything below is created and kept on your devices. None of it is sent to us.

2.1 Motion sensor readings

While a set is running, the App reads the watch's accelerometer (acceleration, in m/s²) and gyroscope (rotation, in rad/s) about 50 times a second. The readings are processed in real time on the watch to count reps and to see how the reps slowed down. In a normal workout the raw readings are not stored: only the results below are kept. The sensors are not read outside a set.

2.2 Workouts

For each workout the App keeps:

  • the start and end time of the workout and of each set;
  • the movement of each set (pull-up, chin-up, push-up, dip, squat, goblet squat or sit-up);
  • the number of reps the watch counted, and the number after your correction;
  • the weight you set (weight lifted, or weight added to a body-weight movement);
  • how the set felt (Easy, Solid or Maxed), whether you picked it or left the watch's guess, how much the reps slowed down, and how long each rep took.

2.3 Settings the App learns

  • Calibration: small adjustments per movement, learnt from your corrections, that make the rep counter and the effort guess fit you better.
  • Preferences: the movements you picked last time and the last weight you used for each movement, so the next workout starts where you left off.

2.4 Recorded sets (optional)

If you use Record a set, the App saves every sensor reading of that one set, the movement, and how many reps you say you really did, as a CSV file. This feature exists only to help tune the rep counter. It is never started without you choosing it, and you can Discard a recording before it is saved.

2.5 What the App does not collect

The App does not collect or have access to:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account details (there is no account);
  • your location or GPS;
  • heart rate, SpO₂, sleep, stress or other health readings from the watch;
  • contacts, messages, photos, the microphone or the camera;
  • advertising identifiers, device identifiers or usage analytics.

3. Permissions

Permission Why the App needs it
Accelerometer To count reps of movements where the wrist travels with the body (for example a squat).
Gyroscope To count reps of movements where the forearm turns (for example a pull-up or a push-up).
Vibration To buzz once for every rep counted and when the rest timer ends.

The App requests no network, location, health, contacts or storage permission.

4. How the data is used

The data is used only to provide the App's features to you:

  • counting reps and estimating the effort of each set;
  • keeping a workout in progress safe if the App is closed (Resume workout);
  • showing the summary of a workout, and starting the next one from your last movements and weights (Again);
  • improving the counter and the effort guess for you, on your watch;
  • sending finished workouts and recorded sets to the FlexFit app on your phone.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, selling, or any purpose other than the above.

5. Where the data goes

5.1 From the watch to your phone

When you Save a workout, it is placed in an outbox on the watch. The App sends waiting files to the FlexFit app on your phone using HUAWEI Wear Engine, through the Huawei Health app that pairs your watch with your phone. Wear Engine only delivers the files to the phone app whose package name and signing certificate match FlexFit's, so another app cannot pose as it. Once the phone confirms that it received a file, the App deletes it from the watch.

If phone sync is not set up, or the phone app is not running, finished workouts simply wait on the watch.

5.2 On your phone

  • Workouts are added to the FlexFit phone app's own log as "Watch workout", in the app's private storage.
  • Recorded sets are saved as they are to Download/FlexFit/recordings/, so that you can copy them to a computer. Files in this folder can be seen by file managers and by other apps you allow to read your Downloads folder.

The FlexFit phone app has its own privacy policy, which governs what happens to your data on the phone.

5.3 Huawei

The connection between the watch and the phone is provided by Huawei (Huawei Health, Wear Engine and, on a phone that is not a Huawei, HMS Core). Huawei handles that connection under its own terms and privacy statement: consumer.huawei.com/en/privacy/privacy-policy. We do not control Huawei's processing and do not receive any data from Huawei about you. The App is distributed through HUAWEI AppGallery, which may process download and installation information under Huawei's own policies.

5.4 Us

We receive nothing from the App. The only way information about you can reach us is if you choose to contact us, for example by emailing us a question or sending us recorded sets to help improve the counter. In that case we use what you send only to answer you or to tune the counter, we keep it only as long as needed for that, and we delete it when you ask. Recorded sets we use for tuning are kept without your name or email address.

5.5 No selling or sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with anyone, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics services.

6. Legal basis (EEA, UK and similar laws)

Where data protection laws require a legal basis, the processing done by the App on your devices is based on:

  • Performance of a contract — providing the rep counting, workout logging and phone sync you ask the App to provide;
  • Consent — for the sensor permissions you grant, and for recorded sets, which you start yourself. You can withdraw consent at any time (see Your data subject rights);
  • Legitimate interest — for replying to messages you send us.

Workout information can reveal something about your physical fitness. Because it stays on your devices and is processed only to give you the features you use, you keep full control over it at all times.

7. Data retention and deletion

Data Kept until
Workout in progress You finish it (a workout without sets is dropped) — it then moves to the outbox.
Finished workouts and recorded sets on the watch The phone confirms it received them, then they are deleted from the watch.
Calibration and preferences You uninstall the App or clear its data.
Workouts and recordings on the phone You delete them in the FlexFit phone app or from Download/FlexFit/recordings/, or uninstall the phone app.
Messages you send us Your question is answered or your request is completed, and in any case no longer than 12 months, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

To delete everything on the watch, uninstall FlexFit Watch from the watch (or from the Huawei Health app). The watch removes all of the App's files with it.

8. Security

The App stores its files in its own private storage on the watch, which other apps cannot read. Transfers to the phone go only through Wear Engine to the verified FlexFit phone app. Because the App has no internet access, no data leaves your devices through it. The safety of your data also depends on your devices: we recommend a screen lock on your phone and a PIN on your watch, and keeping both up to date.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, and withdrawing consent. Most of them you can exercise directly in the App and on your phone, because that is where your data is. The full list, and how to exercise each right, is on our Data Subject Rights page.

10. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, such as 16 in some EEA countries, or 18 under Indonesian law without a parent's or guardian's consent). We do not knowingly receive data from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. International users

The App works the same way everywhere. Because your data stays on your watch and phone, the App itself makes no cross-border transfer of your data. Huawei's services may process connection data in other countries under Huawei's own policies. If you email us, your message is handled through our email provider, which may store it outside your country.

12. Not a medical device

Rep counts and effort estimates are guesses from motion sensors, meant to help you log your training. They are not medical advice and the App is not a medical device.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, the law or our practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it last changed. If a change affects how your data is handled in a meaningful way, we will say so in the App's release notes on HUAWEI AppGallery.
