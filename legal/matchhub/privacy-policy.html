Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Short version: MatchHub is designed to work fully offline. Your tournaments, participants, and scores are stored only on your device. We do not run accounts, we do not have a server, and we do not collect or sell your personal information. The only data leaving your device is what third‑party services (ads and app‑store purchases) require, as described below.

1.Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how the MatchHub mobile application (the “App”) handles information. It applies to the App on all platforms it is distributed on. It does not cover any third‑party websites or services that the App links to.

2.Data you create stays on your device

Everything you enter into MatchHub — tournament names, participant names, seeds, match scores, standings, and settings such as language and theme — is stored locally on your device only, using the operating system's standard local storage. This data:

  • is never uploaded to us or to any external server we control;
  • is not shared between devices unless you deliberately share it (see Section 4);
  • is removed when you delete a tournament, use “Clear All Data” in Settings, or uninstall the App.

Because this data lives only on your device, we cannot recover it for you if the device is lost or the App is uninstalled. Consider exporting or noting important results yourself.

3.Information we do not collect

MatchHub has no user accounts and no analytics that we operate. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, or device identifiers for our own use.

4.Local sharing features

MatchHub includes features that share a tournament with nearby devices over your local network or personal hotspot (for example, the “Local Hotspot Share” viewer and casting to a secondary display). When you turn these on:

  • tournament data is served directly from your device to other devices on the same local network;
  • the data does not pass through us or the public internet;
  • anyone on that local network who has the address can view the shared tournament while sharing is active. Only enable it on networks you trust, and turn it off when you are done.

5.Advertising

The free version of MatchHub displays ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and process certain information (such as advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, and device/usage data) in accordance with their own policies.

  • Google's practices are described in the Google Privacy & Terms.
  • You can review or reset your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • Purchasing the one‑time Remove Ads option disables ad requests within the App.

6.In‑app purchases

MatchHub offers a one‑time purchase to remove ads. Payments are processed entirely by the platform app store (Apple App Store or Google Play), not by us. We never see or store your payment details. The store shares with us only the minimal receipt information needed to confirm and restore your purchase. Purchases are governed by the applicable store's terms and refund policy.

7.Children's privacy

MatchHub is a general‑audience utility and is not directed at children under the age required by your local law for consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we do not collect personal data, any information in the App is whatever you choose to type in.

8.Your choices

  • Delete your data at any time via Settings → Clear All Data, or by uninstalling the App.
  • Limit ad tracking using your device's privacy settings.
  • Go ad‑free with the one‑time Remove Ads purchase.

9.Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the App after changes take effect means you accept the revised policy.
