Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Your trip data stays on your device. Expense records, budgets, and notes are stored locally and never sent to our servers. Optional AI features require a network connection and process only what you explicitly submit.

1. Information We Collect

KeepTrip stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Trip names, destinations, and dates
  • Expense amounts, categories, and notes
  • Budget settings and currency preferences

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the Export feature or opt-in to AI features (see Section 3).

2. Currency Exchange Rates

To provide real-time exchange rates, the app makes anonymous requests to the open.er-api.com API. No personal data is sent — only the currency code you request. These requests are also cached locally so you can use the app fully offline.

3. AI Features & Device Identifier

KeepTrip offers optional AI-powered features (itinerary generation, receipt scanning, and photo itinerary import). When you use these features:

  • Content you submit — receipt images, destination names, and travel preferences are sent to our secure backend (Supabase) and processed by Google Gemini API to generate results. This content is used solely to produce the AI response and is not retained or used for profiling.
  • Credit balance — your usage is tracked server-side against a credit balance linked to a device identifier (see below) to enforce the fair-use daily limit.

Device Identifier (Android)

On Android, we use the Android ID — a system-generated identifier provided by Android — to manage your AI credit balance and prevent fraudulent creation of multiple free accounts. This falls under fraud prevention, one of the explicitly permitted uses of Android ID under Google's developer guidelines.

Key privacy points:

  • No special permission is required to read Android ID.
  • It is scoped to this app and its signing key — other apps see a different value for the same device.
  • It is not used for advertising — advertising uses the separate, user-resettable Advertising ID.
  • It is not shared with third parties — stored only in our Supabase database for account management.
  • It cannot be used to identify you personally.

Device Identifier (iOS & other platforms)

On iOS and other platforms, a randomly generated UUID is stored locally on your device and used for the same account-management purpose. This ID is not linked to any hardware identifier.

4. Advertising

The free version of KeepTrip may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can remove all ads by upgrading to KeepTrip Pro.

5. In-App Purchases

Purchase transactions are processed entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play. KeepTrip does not process or store any payment information. If you purchase AI credits or a Pro subscription, a purchase token is verified with our backend to credit your account — no payment details are transmitted or stored by us.

6. Data Export

You can export your data as a JSON file at any time via Settings → Export Backup. You are responsible for the security of exported files.

7. Children's Privacy

KeepTrip is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
